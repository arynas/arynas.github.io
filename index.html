Selected work

Making small models work for Indonesian agentic AI

Case study · 2025–2026

How we evaluated and adapted small local models for Indonesian function calling: no-tool behavior, tool choice, arguments and relative dates, plus the deployment trade-offs of quantization, backend and hardware.

Agentic model evaluation program

  • Led and directly contributed to an internal evaluation with 51 populated model and configuration sheets, covering no-tool behavior, tool selection, arguments, date reasoning, quantization, backends and CPU/GPU trade-offs.
  • Kept function-call generation separate from end-to-end task completion.

Medical AI and multimodal training

  • Designed and reviewed a Direct versus Chain-of-Thought evaluation across 45 bilingual medical scenarios. Both modes completed 45 of 45, and average total time rose from 28.41 s to 55.66 s with CoT. Completion did not prove medical correctness.
  • Led and contributed to a two-stage bilingual medical VLM pipeline (QLoRA and LoRA-MoE adapters) on a 24 GB GPU. Diagnosed adapter-freezing errors, gradient-checkpointing and graph-retention issues, loss scaling, masked labels, BF16 NaN and OOM. Smoke tests and an initial canary run completed; full end-to-end training is not yet complete.
  • Led multimodal experiment iterations from about 300 to about 36,000 examples, diagnosing under-training, metric defects, label noise and plateaus.

Indonesian speech AI and machine translation

  • Evaluated four Indonesian ASR configurations on the MEDISCO set (360 prediction rows and 4,553 reference words per file). Normalized WER ranged from 2.88% to 4.09%.
  • Worked across Wav2Vec2-XLSR, Whisper, Qwen3-ASR and Qwen3-TTS for data preparation, adaptation, evaluation, quantized inference and backend comparison.
  • Led translation work with MarianMT, M2M-100 and GemmaX-style CPT and SFT, and documented EOS, repetition, numeric-corruption and wrong-language failures.

Research practice and model stewardship

  • Identified train-test leakage and out-of-domain failures hidden by strong validation scores, and used them to limit model claims and plan follow-up tests.
  • Guided NER and sentiment systems from CRF, BiLSTM and TensorFlow/Keras to PyTorch and IndoBERT, driven by user edits and error analysis.
  • Set team practices for data splits, cross-validation, reproducible tests, changelogs and written experiment reviews.
  • Built a research knowledge base of 55 topic sheets and an architecture review of 18 model families.
